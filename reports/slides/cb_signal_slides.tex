\frame{\titlepage}

\begin{frame}{Motivation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Central bank tone --- hawkish vs.\ dovish --- is argued to carry
    information beyond the policy decision itself.
  \item Question: does a systematic, text-derived tone signal, built with
    openly documented methods and tested on \textbf{real market data},
    produce a backtestable edge once transaction costs are applied?
  \item We answer this plainly, including when the answer is ``not
    robustly.''
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Data}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{3,968 real documents}, 1997--2026, scraped directly from
    issuing institutions:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item ECB: 1,871 speeches
    \item Fed: 1,649 (statements, minutes, speeches)
    \item BoE: 448 (speeches, MPC minutes)
  \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rates}: 2Y/10Y US Treasury, DE/UK long yields, VIX --- FRED,
    1956--2026 (39,665 obs)
  \item All three central-bank scrapers required real fixes this cycle:
    ECB/BoE listing pages moved to JS-rendered search widgets; Fed's
    meeting-date discovery had a pre-existing bug mislabelling
    minutes-release dates as meeting dates.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Three independent sentiment tiers}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dictionary}: curated hawkish/dovish lexicon
    (Apel \& Blix Grimaldi 2012; Hansen \& McMahon 2016; Bennani \&
    Neuenkirch 2017) + negation handling.
  \item \textbf{FinBERT} (ProsusAI): pretrained financial-sentiment
    transformer, chunked \& averaged over long documents.
  \item \textbf{Fine-tuned tier}: MiniLM sentence embeddings + logistic
    regression, trained on a small \emph{author-curated} (not
    human-annotated) 482-example set.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.5em}
Cross-checking three independently-built methods makes the dictionary
baseline's results harder to dismiss as a lexicon artifact.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Signal construction and backtest}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Weekly hawkish index $\rightarrow$ 12-week EMA baseline
    $\rightarrow$ expanding-window z-scored surprise (point-in-time by
    construction).
  \item Tradable signal: 4-week rolling blend of level \& surprise,
    applied to 2Y/10Y bond-return proxies.
  \item 20bps round-trip transaction costs, $T{+}1$ execution.
  \item \textbf{Found \& fixed}: the original regime-labelling HMM was fit
    once on the full sample (look-ahead for that label). A walk-forward
    refit changes 45\% of weekly labels. It doesn't affect the backtest
    P\&L --- regime is descriptive only --- but it did affect how
    trustworthy the regime plot was.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Full-sample results: no robust edge}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Strategy & Ann.\ return & Sharpe & Max DD & Turnover/yr \\
\midrule
Short 10Y & $+0.07\%$ & $0.07$  & $-6.1\%$  & 2.2 \\
50/50     & $-0.24\%$ & $-0.40$ & $-10.0\%$ & 2.9 \\
Short 2Y  & $-0.55\%$ & $-1.61$ & $-15.2\%$ & 3.6 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{1em}

Weaker than the synthetic-data version of this same pipeline (Sharpe up
to 1.75) --- expected, since synthetic tone is constructed to drive
returns.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{But: the signal works in one specific regime}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Period & Strategy & Sharpe \\
\midrule
GFC (2007--09)   & 50/50 & $-1.35$ \\
ZIRP (2009--15)  & 50/50 & $-1.18$ \\
COVID (2020--21) & 50/50 & $-1.01$ \\
\textbf{Hiking (2022--23)} & \textbf{50/50} & \textbf{1.83} \\
Plateau (2024+)  & 50/50 & $-1.12$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{1em}

Hypothesis: tone carries more information when policy stance is actually
\emph{changing} quickly, less when anchored (ZIRP) or shock-dominated
(COVID). One episode --- suggestive, not proven.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Negative-to-flat full-sample result --- reported because it's
    true.
  \item Fine-tuned tier's training set is small and author-curated, not
    independently annotated.
  \item Sub-period finding rests on a single hiking cycle.
  \item Constant-duration bond-return proxy, not live futures/contract
    specs (2Y leg likely uneconomic at 20bps TC on cash bonds; futures
    round-trips are closer to 1bp).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
